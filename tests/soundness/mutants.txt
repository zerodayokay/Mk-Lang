# never in the tree.
# Blocks: `mutant <id>`, then rule, class (R restrictive, P permissive),
# file, anchor (text that occurs exactly once in the file), replace, note,
# and `end`. anchor and replace take \n, \t and \\ escapes. A row with
# `pending <why>` names a rule whose site is not anchored yet: it counts for
